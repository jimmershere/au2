Resurrection

1957 Bel Air · Full Restoration · Currently In The Shop

RESURRECTION — in primer, bay 2, July 2026

The Story

Back From The Dead

Some cars earn their name. This 1957 Bel Air sat in a Leelanau County pole barn for over thirty years — floors gone, quarters gone, mice in everything. Most shops would have called it a parts car. Its owner called us.

RESURRECTION is exactly what it sounds like: a ground-up revival of one of the most iconic American cars ever built. We're deep in the metalwork now — fabricating what can't be saved, preserving every panel that can, and documenting each step for the owner along the way.

This page is a living build journal. We update it as the car moves through the shop, so check back — the next entry might be color in the booth.

The Journal

Build Log

Journal 1 — Teardown, rust mapping

Feb 2026

Teardown & Rust Assessment

Full disassembly and cataloguing. Media-blasted the shell to see what thirty years in a barn really left us — floors and lower quarters are gone, but the roof, doors, and iconic trim lines are savable.

Journal 2 — New floors welded in

Apr 2026

Metal Fabrication

New floor pans, trunk pan, and inner quarters fabricated and welded in. Where reproduction panels didn't fit our standard, we made our own. The shell is structurally whole again for the first time in decades.

Journal 3 — Panel fit & gapping

Jun 2026

Bodywork & Panel Fit

Hundreds of hours of hammer-and-dolly work, skim coats, and block sanding. Doors, hood, and trunk hung and gapped — tighter than Flint managed in 1957. The famous fins are laser straight.

Journal 4 — In primer, drivetrain mockup

Jul 2026

In Primer, Drivetrain Mockup

The body is sealed in primer and blocked for paint. Meanwhile the rebuilt V8 and transmission are in mockup — checking clearances and fitment before final assembly. Color is next. Stay tuned.
