# Project Mako — 2006 Hyundai Tiburon GT V6 Import Resto-Mod

*Tiburón* is Spanish for shark, so the build name is **Mako**, after the fastest
shark. This is an internal buy-or-pass analysis and build plan for Appearance
Unlimited. It is **not site content**, and `deploy.sh` excludes this folder.

> All prices are 2026 estimates for parts and materials at DIY cost. Shop labor
> is ours, so it is counted in **hours**, not dollars. Check every number against
> a live quote before ordering. Nothing here comes from an in-person inspection.
> The inspection checklist in section 3 decides the final offer.

## 2. What it's worth as it sits

A car that has sat for four months, is missing a headlight, has flat tires and an
unknown run status is priced as a **project car**, not a driver.

| Scenario | Estimated value |
|---|---|
| Clean, running 2006 Tiburon GT V6 auto, average miles (private party, MI) | $2,500 – $4,000 |
| **As it sits** (runs after a battery, no headlight, flat tires, sat 4 months) | **$1,200 – $2,200** |
| Doesn't run, or title/rust problems | $600 – $1,200 |
| Scrap floor (~2,900 lb curb weight) | about $250 – $400 |

**Offer strategy**

- **Open at $1,000 – $1,200** cash, as-is, and point to the headlight, the tires, the
  four months of sitting and the timing-belt unknown.
- **Walk-away ceiling: $2,000**, and only if it starts, the transmission shifts
  cleanly, there is no rot in the rockers or strut towers, and the boxed parts are
  useful.
- Automatic cars sell for less than the 6-speed manual GTs. Use that in the negotiation.
- Mileage is unknown. Get the odometer reading before naming a number. Over about
  180k miles with no timing-belt record, drop the offer by $300 – $500.

## 3. Pre-purchase inspection (do this before the offer)

1. **Title**: clean and in the seller's name, with no salvage/rebuilt brand. Run the VIN at
   nhtsa.gov/recalls for open recalls.
2. **Cold start**: listen for timing-belt or tensioner noise and a lifter tick. Check for
   smoke and read codes with an OBD-II scanner.
3. **Timing belt**: the Delta 2.7 is an **interference engine**. With no receipt,
   treat the belt as due. A snapped belt means bent valves.
4. **Pre-cats**: rattling or failing front catalysts are a known 2.7 V6 weak spot.
   Tap the manifolds and listen.
5. **Transmission**: check that the ATF is red, not burnt. Drive it through all four gears
   and Shiftronic, and watch for flare on the 2→3 shift.
6. **Rust (Michigan car)**: rear wheel-arch lips, rockers under the plastic
   skirts, rear subframe mounts, strut towers, floor pans under the carpet.
7. **Water**: lift the carpet at the front footwells, look for a wet headliner at the sunroof corners,
   and check the spare-tire well.
8. **Front impact**: check the driver headlight tabs, radiator core support, hood-latch
   alignment and bumper absorber.
9. **Oil leaks**: check the valve-cover and spark-plug tube seals (common), and the rear main.
10. **Motor mounts**: the Tiburon's are weak. Check for engine rock during an
    in-gear brake-torque test.
11. **Boxes in the back seat**: open them and list what's inside.

## 4. Design direction

**Concept: a modern Hyundai N look on a 2000s Hyundai sport coupe.** Keep it
unmistakably a Tiburon and don't hide the badge. Borrow the look of the current
Veloster N and Elantra N so the car looks like the ancestor it is.

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Body color (recommended)** | **Hyundai N "Performance Blue"**, a current factory Hyundai color and code, so it's easy to match later |
| Accent | Gloss black roof, mirror caps and rear spoiler. **Red N-style pinstripe** on the front lip and side-skirt edges, which ties into the red stitching already in the cabin |
| Alt. colorway B | Refreshed OE silver (code TW) with gloss-black accents. Cheapest, and no jambs to shoot |
| Alt. colorway C | A metallic blue-gray fading to a pale belly. A nod to the 1961 Corvette "Mako Shark" concept. Most labor, biggest show impact |
| Stance | Coilovers, about 1.25–1.5" drop, flush 18s, mild negative camber. Street-usable in Northern Michigan with no scraping |
| Lighting | Matched projector headlights (solves the missing lamp), smoked LED tails, LED fogs |
| Aero | Lose the textured wing. Use a low lip spoiler or refinished OE GT spoiler, a front splitter lip, side-skirt extensions and a small rear diffuser |
| Interior | Keep the OE black/red theme, but restore it: re-skinned bolster, Alcantara wheel with red 12-o'clock stripe, new carpet, modern head unit |

### Phase 3 — Power (Delta 2.7 V6)

Be realistic. The Delta 2.7 has limited aftermarket support and no mainstream tune
exists for the 2006 automatic. Bolt-ons will give **about +15–25 whp and much
better sound**, not big numbers. Build it for response and noise.

| Part | Recommendation | Est. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Intake | Short-ram or cold-air intake | $150 – $300 |
| Headers | Tiburon GT V6 headers (Fast Intentions made a line for this car). Replaces the failure-prone pre-cats | $500 – $900 |
| Cats | **Federal-compliant high-flow cats. Not test pipes.** Michigan has no emissions test, but federal anti-tampering rules still apply and it keeps the CEL off | $300 – $600 |
| Cat-back | Stainless 2.25–2.5" dual cat-back with resonator (no drone on the highway) | $400 – $900 |
| Trans cooler | Auxiliary ATF cooler. **Required** if it stays automatic and gets driven hard | $80 – $150 |
| Ignition | OE-spec coils if any are weak (check at Phase 0) | $0 – $300 |
| **Phase 3 total** | | **$1,430 – $3,150** |
| **Shop hours** | | **10 – 16 h** |

**Optional: 6-speed manual swap** from a donor Tiburon GT (transmission,
clutch/flywheel, pedal box, clutch master and slave, shifter and cables, manual ECU
and immobilizer pairing, wiring). **$1,500 – $3,500 and 25–40 h.** This is the
single biggest change to how the car drives and what it's worth, because manual
GTs sell for noticeably more. Only do it if a complete donor turns up locally.

Not recommended: turbo or supercharger kits for the Delta. Support is thin,
parts are hard to find, and the 4-speed auto won't take the torque.

## 6. Rolled-up budget

| Build level | Phases | Parts & materials | Shop hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Street Clean**: running, safe, stanced, OE-silver respray | 0 + 1 + budget 2 + 4 (colorway B) + light 5, no aero | **$7,000 – $12,000** | 100 – 150 h |
| **Mako (recommended)**: everything above, Performance Blue, bolt-ons, full interior | 0 – 5 | **$10,100 – $20,700** | 150 – 240 h |
| **Mako + 6-speed** | 0 – 5 + manual swap | **$11,600 – $24,200** | 175 – 280 h |

**Add the purchase price** ($1,000 – $2,000), plus Michigan title, tax and plate
(6% sales tax on the purchase price plus title and registration fees, roughly $150 – $300).

**All-in, realistic middle of the Mako build: about $17,000 cash** (about $15,400 in parts plus purchase and fees).

### Plan honestly for these

- **Labor isn't free.** 150–240 h of bay time at the shop's customer rate is the
  real cost of this car. Schedule it for slow weeks.
- **Resale won't cover the build.** A finished Tiburon GT resto-mod will
  probably sell for **$6,000 – $9,000** (more with a 6-speed and good photos). Treat
  it as a **showcase and marketing build**, not a flip.
- **Contingency:** hold **15%** for surprises: rust, a tired automatic, or broken clips.
