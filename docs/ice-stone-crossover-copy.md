# Ice & Stone × Appearance Unlimited — crossover drop copy

Draft copy for the limited-edition crossover design. **Everything here is drafted
from what jimmer stated on 2026-09-29 and nothing else.** Ice & Stone is described
as "Jimmer's own design shop, under construction" — no launch date, no product
line, no site. Those gaps are left as gaps below rather than filled in; see
"Unverified" at the end before publishing any of it.

## Long version — the "why" (about box / newsletter)

> Small shops in a small town do better when they point customers at each other.
> That's the whole idea behind this drop.
>
> **Appearance Unlimited** does autobody and restoration work in Traverse City —
> the panel gaps, the paint, the cars people actually care about. **Ice & Stone**
> is Jimmer's design shop, still under construction, and it exists to do the
> other half: the artwork, the lettering, the merch.
>
> A crossover drop is the cheapest possible collaboration. No contracts, no
> inventory, no storefront to build. One design, both names, a limited run — and
> two customer lists that get introduced to each other. If it works, we do it
> again with a different local shop. If it doesn't, we've lost a weekend.
>
> *We Deal In Lead* is the first one. Tombstone, 1879, relocated to a Michigan
> winter.

## Positioning notes (internal — do not publish)

- **Why it sits on the AU2 site at all.** The design carries no Appearance
  Unlimited marks, so without the crossover framing it reads as an unrelated
  product on an autobody shop's merch page. The framing *is* the justification —
  drop the framing and the design should come off the site.
- **Limited edition is a claim.** Print-on-demand has no scarcity by default. If
  the listing says limited, decide the actual limit (a date, or a unit count) and
  honour it, or drop the word. Marketplaces treat false scarcity as a
  policy problem, not a turn of phrase.
- **Price.** Drafted at $36 tee / $60 hoodie, a premium over the $32/$55 AU2 line
  to match the limited framing. Arbitrary — change it if the premium isn't wanted.
